# DemoArena art pass — design

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Owner:** Person 2 · **Status:** approved in brainstorming, pending spec review

## Goal

Replace DemoArena's primitive cubes and cylinders with a readable low-poly forest clearing: textured terrain, real rock/log/stump cover sized for the lean-to-peek mechanic, a tree-line boundary, fog. Gameplay code and the Player / Pea / TargetDummy prefabs are untouched.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Scene | Upgrade `DemoArena.unity` (not ForestArena, which Person 3 owns). |
| Art source | CC0 downloads: **Kenney Nature Kit 2.1** models + **Poly Haven** 1k textures. |
| Style | Low-poly flat-colour props on a PBR-textured Unity Terrain, plus fog. |
| Player models | Out of scope (pea player stays primitives). |
| Build method | Editor script `ArenaBuilder` (menu **PeaHeads > Build Arena**), runnable headless via `-executeMethod`. |

## ArenaBuilder (`Editor/ArenaBuilder.cs`)

Static class in the `PeaHeads.Editor` asmdef (namespace `PeaHeads.EditorTools`), same style as `NetTestSceneBuilder`.

1. Opens `Scenes/DemoArena.unity`.
2. Destroys the `Environment` root and everything under it, recreates it empty. **Nothing outside `Environment` is created or deleted** (NetworkManager, SpawnPoints, OverviewCamera, Directional Light, ConnectionUI stay).
3. Sets texture importers: `*_nor_gl_*` → Normal map. Creates/overwrites 3 `TerrainLayer` assets (tile size 4 m).
4. Creates/overwrites `ArenaTerrain.asset` (TerrainData) and a Terrain + TerrainCollider under `Environment`:
   - 80 × 80 m, centred on origin, height range 10 m, heightmap 129², alphamap 128².
   - Flat at y = 0 inside |x|, |z| ≤ 21. Outside, height ramps up smoothly (Perlin noise added) to 3–6 m by the edge, so the clearing reads as a bowl.
   - Paint: grass everywhere; dirt path ~3 m wide (noise-wiggled) from spawn to spawn along x = 0 plus a cross path along z = 0; forest floor outside the play area.
5. Places props from a layout table in the script (one `Place(model, x, z, yRotation, targetHeight, collider)` per row).
   - Each prop is uniformly scaled so its renderer bounds height equals `targetHeight` (Kenney scale is inconsistent; this makes the table readable in metres).
   - Cover gets a static non-convex `MeshCollider` per mesh. Decoration gets none.
6. Boundary: 4 invisible `BoxCollider`s (3 m high) on the old wall lines (±20), no renderers. Two to three staggered rows of trees outside, at |x| or |z| between 22 and 34, positions from a seeded `System.Random(42)` so reruns are identical.
7. Decoration: ~40 grass/bush/mushroom pieces scattered (seeded) inside the play area, skipping paths, spawns (3 m radius) and cover footprints.
8. Render settings: linear fog, colour = camera background `(0.62, 0.8, 0.95)`, start 25 m, end 70 m; gradient ambient light; sun warmed slightly (colour + intensity on the existing `Directional Light`, found by name).
9. Saves the scene and assets.

Re-running is safe but **overwrites any hand edits under `Environment`**. Hand-tune either by editing the layout table and re-running, or by editing the scene and never re-running.

## Layout

Play area unchanged: 40 × 40 m, spawns at (0, 0, −12) and (0, 0, 12).

Cover heights come from the player: head centre at 1.7 m, top at ~2.15 m, lean up to 0.6 m sideways.
- **Tall cover** ≥ 2.3 m hides a standing head. Width 1.5–3 m, so leaning out exposes it.
- **Low cover** ~1.1 m hides the body only (base/stem hittable from above angles, head exposed).

Layout is **point-symmetric about the origin**: every row at (x, z, rot) is mirrored to (−x, −z, rot + 180) so neither spawn has an advantage. Starting table (half; tune after playtest):

| Model | x | z | rot | height | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rock_tallA` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2.6 | centre (self-symmetric, placed once) |
| `rock_largeA` | 2.5 | −8 | 15 | 2.4 | spawn shield |
| `log_large` | −6 | −5 | 20 | 1.1 | low cover |
| `stump_oldTall` | 6 | −3 | 0 | 2.4 | mid tall |
| `rock_largeD` | −11 | −2 | 40 | 2.5 | left flank |
| `log_stackLarge` | 11 | −9 | 90 | 1.2 | right flank, low |
| `rock_tallB` | −5 | −13 | 0 | 2.5 | spawn side |
| `stump_round` | 13 | 2 | 0 | 1.0 | low |
| `tree_oak` | −15 | −14 | 0 | 6 | corner tree, collider |

## Unchanged

Player, Pea, TargetDummy prefabs; all scripts outside `Editor/`; NetTest scene and builder; `Art/Nature` and `Art/Materials` (no longer referenced by DemoArena, but kept).

## Verification

- Headless: run `ArenaBuilder.Build` via `-batchmode -executeMethod` with the editor closed; it must compile, log no errors, and save the scene. EditMode tests still pass.
- Playtest (human; I can't check visuals):
  - Peas stop on rocks, logs, stumps and terrain.
  - Players can't leave the 40 × 40 area; no snagging on paths.
  - Leaning out from tall cover exposes the head; standing behind it hides it.
  - Both spawns feel equal.
  - Frame rate is fine on the weaker laptop.

## Out of scope

Player/pea model upgrade, terrain sculpting by hand, skybox, water, audio ambience, ForestArena.
